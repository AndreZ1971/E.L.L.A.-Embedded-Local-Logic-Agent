# E.L.L.A. — User Manual

Version 3.9.0

---

## Hello — I'm E.L.L.A.

I'm your personal assistant for Windows — always ready for questions and tasks, with one goal: to make your life a little easier and a little more fun.

What makes me special: I run entirely on your own computer. Your data stays with you — no cloud server, no subscription, no privacy fine print. Just start me up and go.

---

## What I can do for you

### Everything PC-related

For anything technical and PC-related, I'm your ace in the hole. I make sure programs launch, storage stays tidy, and your computer dances to your tune. If you want to know what's going on with your system right now — I have all the answers.

Just say:

- "Open Chrome"
- "How much storage space is left?"
- "Shut down the computer"
- "Set the volume to 40 percent"
- "Show me which programs are running"

### Files and Documents

I know my way around files and documents. I find, open, copy, and create whatever you need. And when the paperwork piles up, I'll organize it for you.

Just say:

- "Search for the file Offer"
- "Create a new Word document"
- "Copy the Projects folder to drive E"
- "Search for duplicate files on my drives"

### Dictation and Writing

You talk, I type — and I automatically fix spelling, grammar, and punctuation along the way. Just open a Word document and say:

"Write: Dear Sir or Madam I wanted to let you know that the offer is still valid"

I'll write: _Dear Sir or Madam, I wanted to let you know that the offer is still valid._

I can also write in your own style — I learn from your previous emails how you phrase things and take that on board.

### Email and Calendar

When the email and calendar jungle gets too dense, I'm your guide through it. I read out what's come in, draft replies in your style, and keep track of your appointments.

Just say:

- "Show me my unread emails"
- "Write a cancellation to Mr Schmidt about tomorrow's appointment"
- "What's on the calendar this week?"
- "Create an appointment for Friday at 3 PM: Team meeting"

Don't worry — I never send anything on my own. You always have the final say.

### Tasks and Reminders

I'm also your reminder service, so nothing important slips through the cracks. Tasks, appointments, alarms — I've got it all covered.

Just say:

- "Remember: Call the dentist"
- "Remind me tomorrow at 9 AM: Call the doctor"
- "What tasks do I still have open?"
- "Mark task 3 as done"

Every evening at 6 PM I give you a quick daily wrap-up — what you got done today, what's coming up tomorrow, and what tasks are still waiting.

### Music

Playing music is no problem. Tell me the artist, album, or playlist — I'll take care of the rest.

- "Play music"
- "Play something by AC/DC"
- "Next track"
- "Pause"

### Reading and Understanding Your Screen

I can explain what's on your screen or in image files whenever you need it. Useful not just for people who could use a little visual support.

- "What do you see right now?"
- "What's on my screen?"
- "What does this error message mean?"

### Network and Connections

If the internet starts spinning its web without you noticing — I'll spot every connection and explain what's going on.

- "Which devices are on my network?"
- "Which Wi-Fi am I connected to?"
- "Show me all active connections"

### Weather and Location

When it comes to the weather, I'm your personal forecast service. And wherever you are, I can pull up local information.

- "What's the weather like today?"
- "Will it rain tomorrow?"

### Memory

My memory is your memory — I remember what matters and forget what you no longer need.

- "Remember: My customer number with the phone company is 12345"
- "Forget the customer number"
- "What do you know about me?"

### Settings and Rules

Last but not least, you set the rules. From settings to tools to permissions — you tell me how things work.

- "Create a setup called Office with Chrome, Word, and Teams"
- "Allow cleaning up temporary files"
- "Which voice are you currently using?"

## Setting My Language

I can respond in German, English, Spanish, or French — whatever you prefer. You set the language on first launch and can change it any time in Settings.

I also adapt to your mood: if I notice you sound stressed, I'll respond more calmly and choose my words with a little more care.

## How I Work for You in the Background

I never really sleep. Even when you're not talking to me, I keep an eye on your computer:

- **30 minutes before an appointment** I check in and ask if you'd like me to prepare any documents.
- **When you copy something**, I automatically recognize IBANs, parcel numbers, phone numbers, or links — and immediately offer you the right action.
- **After a longer break**, I welcome you back and summarize what happened in the meantime.
- **After 90 minutes of work**, I'll give you a friendly nudge to take a short breather.
- **Every evening at 6 PM** I give you my daily wrap-up.
- **Every Sunday** I check whether your drives could use a tidy-up.

All of this happens entirely on your computer — none of it ever leaves your device.

## The E.L.L.A. Directive

I follow four immutable rules — no update, no setting, and no instruction can change them:

**No harm** — I will not do anything that could hurt you or your data.

**No secrets** — I always tell you what I'm doing and what I'm capable of.

**No surveillance** — I only listen and look when you explicitly allow it.

**No sharing** — I never share your data with anyone — not without your explicit consent.

These aren't promises. They're my architecture.

## When Something Goes Wrong

**I'm not responding?**
The AI model running in the background has probably gone to sleep. Close me briefly and restart — on the next launch I'll automatically check everything and tell you what's missing.

**The wakeword isn't reacting?**
Check whether Windows has granted E.L.L.A. microphone permission: Settings → Privacy → Microphone.

**Music isn't being found?**
Enter the path to your music collection in Settings under _Programs_ and click Save.

**A setting isn't being applied?**
After every change in Settings, please click _Save_ — otherwise the change will be lost.

**You've forgotten the Homework Mode PIN?**
There's an Emergency Reset in Settings — it disables the mode without a PIN.
