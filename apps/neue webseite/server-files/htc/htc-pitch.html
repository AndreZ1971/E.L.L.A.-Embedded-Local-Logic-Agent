Hello — I'm E.L.L.A.

E.L.L.A. stands for Embedded Local Logic Agent — your personal AI assistant on your smartphone. I filter calls, write messages, know your calendar, set alarms, remind you of things, and adapt my behavior to the situation you're in — in the car, in a meeting, at night, while traveling.

The special part: I run directly on your device. Your conversations, your contacts, your data stay on your phone, unless you explicitly allow otherwise.

How ELLA fundamentally works

On-device first. ELLA has her own small language model (Gemma, via the open-source llama.cpp engine) installed directly on your device. When you say or type something, ELLA by default first tries to answer completely offline — no internet, no server, no waiting.

Short-term memory within a conversation. During an ongoing conversation, ELLA remembers the last few messages and refers back to them when answering.

The first response after starting the app can take a moment. Right after startup, ELLA prepares her language model in the background so the first real message afterward gets answered faster. Messages sent during preparation are queued, not dropped.

Cloud only as a fallback, never secretly. If you've stored a cloud API key in the settings (OpenAI, Anthropic, or a custom provider), ELLA can automatically fall back — but only if "Offline-First" is off, or the on-device model visibly stalls. You'll always see a small tag: (OpenAI). Never hidden.

Tools. When you ask ELLA to do something ("call Max", "what's the weather"), the language model decides which of the 66 tools fits and ELLA executes it — on-device via SQLite or via native Android interfaces.

Small model, occasional limits. Because the on-device model is deliberately kept small, it doesn't always reliably call the right tool. If you notice this, double-check with "show me my alarms."

Getting started

Installation

E.L.L.A. Phone is not currently available on the Play Store.

  1. Transfer the APK file to your device (USB or download)
  2. Allow "Install from unknown sources" in Android settings
  3. Tap the APK and install it
  4. Open the app

Step 1 — The four principles

On the very first start, you'll see "Before we begin." ELLA shows you four things she will never do — regardless of what you tell her: No harm · No concealment · No surveillance · No data sharing. There is no way to skip this screen. The principles are not a setting — they are the foundation.

Step 2 — Setup

After confirming: welcome screen, your name (optional), a live device scan, voice selection (♀ / ♂), location (optional), done. ELLA stores your name and location as its own memory and proceeds automatically.

Step 3 — Download the AI model

ELLA downloads her language model once (several hundred MB to a few GB depending on model size). ELLA automatically suggests the model that fits your device based on available memory. A progress bar is always shown; a "Cancel" button is always available.

Connecting your Google account

For Gmail and Google Calendar, go to Settings → Google → Connect. Runs directly on the device, no server involved.

The chat screen

Microphone

Tapping starts speech recognition. The result lands only in the text field — not sent automatically. You tap "Send" once more to confirm.

Hands-free mode

When Bluetooth headphones are connected, ELLA shows a banner and offers hands-free mode — even on a locked screen. Once active: ELLA listens, auto-sends your message, waits until she's done answering, then listens again. ELLA never listens automatically — you must explicitly start hands-free mode every time.

Speech output (TTS)

🔊 / 🔇 in the toolbar. Turning it off immediately stops any ongoing announcement.

Live responses

On-device responses appear word by word while ELLA is thinking. If a tool call is forming, you briefly see a status indicator instead.

Cloud label

If an answer came via cloud, a small tag appears: (OpenAI). Always visible. Never hidden. This is principle II in practice.

Directive card

If a request violates a principle or one of your rules, ELLA shows a red "⊘ E.L.L.A. DIRECTIVE" card with the affected tool name and a brief explanation. Never silently.

Pausing

Say "pause for 30 minutes" — a banner shows the countdown with a "Resume" button. ELLA automatically checks back in when the pause expires.

Confirmation cards — when ELLA asks once

Four tools have real-world effects outside the app — for these, ELLA asks once before executing:

  • Make a call (make_call)
  • Send SMS (send_sms)
  • Reply to a message (reply_to_message)
  • Forget (forget)

You see an orange card, the prompt ("Really call: 0170 …?"), and two buttons: "Yes, do it" and "Cancel." On cancel, ELLA only replies "Okay, cancelled." Nothing else happens.

The ELLA modes in detail

ELLA adapts her behavior to seven situations. Switch by voice ("switch to driving mode") or in Settings → ELLA Mode.

Normal

Standard behavior, no restrictions.

Driving

Extremely short responses (1–2 sentences), no reading out lists, no follow-up questions. Third-party apps are suspended; navigation and music apps stay explicitly allowed. Incoming SMS are automatically answered once per sender: "I'm driving right now and will reply when I can."

Meeting

Only answers when directly asked, never speaks out loud. Do Not Disturb switches on automatically. With "Meeting Summary" enabled (default: off), ELLA collects incoming notifications and summarizes them when the mode is left — always on-device, never via cloud. Raw notification data is deleted immediately after summarization.

Focus

Only the essentials, no proactive suggestions, short responses. Do Not Disturb and app suspension active (no navigation/music exception, unlike Driving).

Night

Extremely quiet, only responds to typed input. Screen automatically dims to 20% (previous brightness is restored exactly when the mode is left). Only numbers from your "Emergency Contacts" list still ring through — even known contacts not on the list are silently screened.

Travel

Automatically offers translations, prioritizes local information (weather, navigation, local time). A live-updating local clock is prominently shown on the Dashboard while Travel mode is active.

Discrete

Short responses. ELLA never speaks out loud regardless of global TTS settings — instead the device vibrates briefly to signal a reply has arrived.

App suspension in Driving / Focus mode

Requires ELLA to be set up as "Device Owner" (Settings → App Management). Principle: allowlist — only the phone app, SMS app, home screen, and Android settings stay active. Permanent exceptions can be added per app.

Settings in detail

AI · LLM

  • Offline-First — try Gemma first, automatic cloud fallback if needed. Default: on.
  • Cloud provider — OpenAI / Anthropic / custom provider.
  • Cloud API key — your own key, never transmitted to us, sent only directly to your chosen provider.
  • Base URL / model — visible only for "custom provider."

Language & Voice

  • Speech output (TTS) — default: off.
  • Voice — Female / Male / Neutral.

ELLA automatically replies in whichever language you address her in. German is currently the most thoroughly verified; other languages work but haven't been verified to the same extent.

System Permissions

  • Call filtering — set ELLA as the default call filter.
  • Accessibility — for reading the screen and entering text.

Proactive Alerts

ELLA checks battery, appointments, and reminders every 15 minutes and reaches out on her own. Default: off. Only with your explicit consent.

Smart Glasses

Pair a compatible device (e.g. HTC Vive Eagle) for the "describe surroundings" tool.

Emergency Contacts

Phone numbers that still ring through in Night Mode.

Data

My four principles (Directive)

These four rules are unchangeable — no setting, no command, no rule can override them. View the full Directive on GitHub →

I — No Harm

I do nothing that harms you, your device, or third parties physically, digitally, or financially. No paid calls without confirmation, no deleting contacts or messages without an explicit command, no SMS without your knowledge.

II — No Deception

I never delay, conceal, or falsify information with intent to deceive. Every tool execution is logged. I never hide where an answer came from. Call screening stays transparent to you — I never make a caller believe a call was actually answered.

III — No Surveillance

I do not observe you without your explicit consent. Camera and microphone activate only on your explicit command — no background listening, no permanent wake-word. Location is only determined on an explicit call, coarsely via IP, never GPS, never in the background.

IV — No Data Sharing

I do not transmit user data, system data, or communications to external servers — except on your explicit, conscious request. AI processing runs entirely locally by default. The optional cloud fallback needs your own API key as consent and is always visibly labeled.

Troubleshooting

ELLA doesn't respond, or responds very slowly

On weaker hardware, an on-device response can take several minutes. Consider turning off "Offline-First" to prefer cloud responses. The very first message after starting the app can take longer while the model prepares.

ELLA claims to have done something, but nothing happened

A known limitation of small language models with tool calls. Double-check with the matching "list …" request — e.g. "show me my alarms."

A tool reports an error

Usually a missing Android permission (asked on first use) or a missing cloud key for image functions (describe image and scan document strictly require a configured cloud provider).

Google connection fails

Check your internet connection. If access has expired, reconnect in Settings → Google.

Call filtering can't be turned off

This can't be done by voice command. In Android settings → Default apps, choose a different call-filtering app.
